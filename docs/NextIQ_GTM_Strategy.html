1. Executive Summary

“NextIQ is the AI decision engine that makes every agent your best agent.”

NextIQ is Nextiva's AI-powered agent intelligence engine embedded directly into the NEXT platform. It provides real-time intent classification, Knowledge Base responses, Next Best Action recommendations, tool/action execution, and fully autonomous Autopilot capabilities. NextIQ is sold as a per-seat add-on ($10/seat/month) on Launch and Growth plans, and is included in Enterprise. Billing is consumption-based at the WorkItem level — one interaction equals one unit, regardless of AI complexity.

Three Core Differentiators

🤝
Agent Augmentation
Not bot-first. NextIQ enhances human agents with real-time intelligence, not replaces them. Autopilot is human-in-the-loop autonomous.
🌐
Omnichannel Native
Same AI engine across voice, chat, email, SMS, and social. Channel-adapted delivery — talk tracks for voice, auto-send for digital.
💰
Predictable Pricing
Per-WorkItem billing. No token counting, no per-action fees. A 5-minute check and a 30-minute resolution cost the same — one unit.

Voice Channel Deep Dive

On voice calls, NextIQ cannot send messages to customers directly. Instead, it operates in read-only suggestion mode, presenting intelligence for the agent to communicate verbally. This is a major competitive differentiator — most competitors’ AI assistance is text-only.

Objection Handling

Objection Response
“We already have a chatbot.” NextIQ is not a chatbot. It assists your human agents in real-time, on every channel including voice. Your chatbot handles deflection; NextIQ handles the complex conversations that reach your agents. They’re complementary — chatbot for tier-0, NextIQ for tier-1 and above.
“AI is too expensive and unpredictable.” Unlike competitors that charge per AI resolution ($0.99–$1.00 each), NextIQ bills per WorkItem. A simple billing check and a complex multi-step resolution cost the same — one unit. Free units on every plan let you prove ROI before spending a dollar.
“Our agents won’t use it.” NextIQ is embedded directly into the agent workspace — no context switching, no separate tab. It shows up when relevant and stays out of the way when it’s not. Agents who try it see immediate time savings. Our Phase 1 activation data shows 70%+ weekly active usage among agents who see it once.
“We’re on the Launch plan — is it worth it?” You already get 50 free NextIQ units per month. Start using them. Once your agents experience the value, the $10/seat add-on is the easiest ROI conversation you’ll have. And if you outgrow the add-on, Autopilot on the Growth plan is a natural upgrade path.
“Will AI replace our agents?” The opposite. NextIQ is designed to make agents more effective, not replace them. Even Autopilot mode is human-in-the-loop — agents see what NextIQ plans to do and can intervene at any time. The result is happier agents who handle more complex work, not fewer agents.
“What about data privacy?” NextIQ operates within the Nextiva platform’s existing security and compliance framework. Customer data never leaves the platform perimeter. Actions Builder tools are admin-governed with enable/disable controls. AI model interactions are scoped to the account’s own data.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do we position NextIQ against customers who already have a chatbot?
NextIQ and chatbots are complementary, not competitive. Chatbots handle tier-0 deflection (password resets, FAQ). NextIQ handles the complex interactions that reach human agents — billing disputes, technical troubleshooting, upsell conversations. Position it as: “Your chatbot is the first line. NextIQ makes your second line unstoppable.”
Q: Can we sell NextIQ to Launch plan customers?
Yes. Launch customers get 50 free units and can purchase the $10/seat add-on for full access (excluding Autopilot and Actions Builder, which are Growth+ features). For Launch customers who want Autopilot, position a Growth plan upgrade as the natural path.
Q: What’s the upgrade path from free units to paid?
When free units are exhausted, the admin sees an in-app prompt to either purchase the add-on or buy a unit top-up pack. Agents see a soft-disable banner. Sales is notified via CRM. The conversation is: “Your team loved NextIQ so much they used all 50 units in 12 days. Here’s the AHT improvement they saw. For $10/seat you get unlimited access.”
Q: How does voice channel NextIQ differ from digital?
Same AI intelligence, different delivery. On voice, NextIQ shows suggestions as read-only “talk tracks” for the agent to communicate verbally. The “Send” buttons are replaced with “Mark as Communicated.” Backend actions (tool execution) work identically. See Section 5 for the full channel comparison table.
Q: What industries should we prioritize for NextIQ?
Start with industries that have high interaction volume and complex conversations: SaaS/technology (billing, technical support), financial services (compliance + action execution), healthcare (patient support), and insurance (claims processing). Voice-heavy verticals are particularly strong because competitors don’t serve them well.
Q: How do we handle the “AI will replace agents” objection?
Lead with the agent empowerment story. NextIQ is explicitly designed as an augmentation tool, not a replacement. Even Autopilot is human-in-the-loop — agents see everything NextIQ does and can override it. The result is agents handling more complex, rewarding work while NextIQ handles the cognitive load. Turnover decreases because agents feel supported, not threatened.
Q: What’s the Actions Builder marketplace roadmap?
Phase 1 (now): 10 pre-built action templates (Fetch Account, Check Billing, Process Refund, etc.) available out of the box. Phase 2 (Month 6): Industry-specific action packs (healthcare, finserv, e-commerce). Phase 3 (Month 9): Community-contributed action templates with a marketplace review process. Enterprise customers can publish internal actions across their org.
Q: How does NextIQ fit into the broader Nextiva platform story?
NextIQ is the AI layer of the NEXT platform. Nextiva provides the omnichannel infrastructure (voice, chat, email, SMS, social), the Unified Data Platform (customer context), and now NextIQ as the intelligence engine that connects them. The positioning: “Nextiva NEXT is the platform. NextIQ is the brain. Together, they power conversations that grow your business.”
Q: What about data privacy and compliance?
NextIQ inherits the NEXT platform’s security and compliance posture (SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA eligible, GDPR compliant). Customer data stays within the platform perimeter. AI model interactions are account-scoped. Actions Builder tools are admin-governed with enable/disable controls. No customer data is used to train shared models.
